## 15. purf: Provost's Undergraduate Research Fellowship (PURF)
- category: scholarship | relevance: research
- benefit: stipend of $1,000 for Fall or Spring, $3,000 for Summer (this resolves the earlier disagreement between pages)
- eligibility: undergraduate enrolled in 12 or more units at USC in the grant term (Spring 2027) in a program leading to an undergraduate degree; Progressive Degree students are ineligible; USC cumulative GPA 3.0 or better (preference for 3.5 or better); prior research with a research-active USC faculty member is preferred

- deadline: applications open Tuesday, October 20, 2026; due Tuesday, November 19, 2026, 11:59 pm; decisions announced around mid-January 2027
- source: https://academicprograms.usc.edu/services/undergraduate-research-and-experiential-learning/usc-provosts-undergraduate-research-fellowships/
- key quote: "USC cumulative GPA of 3.0 or better."

- source: https://studentbasicneeds.usc.edu/programs-and-services/food-support/emergency-meal-swipe/
